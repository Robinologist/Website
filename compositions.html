This is the third piece I ever composed in notation, but to this day it is still one of my favorites. My main goal with it was to develop the music in a more natural way than solely stacking and unstacking loops, as tends to be my default approach, and I think I did well to that end. A murmuration is what you call a flock of birds coalesced into one undulating mass, and that's what this piece reminded me of.
